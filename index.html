    Click a square to add a note, drag right to hold it, click it again to erase.

    Auto fill can stay home in one key, or follow a chord progression so the line moves.

    • Only notes in your key are on the roll, so nothing clashes.
    • Click a note name on the left to hear that pitch.
    • The bars under the roll are velocity, how hard each note is played. Click near the top of a bar for hard, the middle for medium, the bottom for soft.
    • Auto fill tries to land on your kick drum, so the bass and kick hit together.
